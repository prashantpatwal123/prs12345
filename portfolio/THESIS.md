## Pre-cleared watchlist

Carried from the September 2026 forensic review. These survived rejection but
are **not** owned. All figures are as of 20 Sep 2026 and must be re-verified
before any purchase — none came from a primary source.

### ONGC (NSE) — watchlist

- Price ₹233 · mcap ₹2,97,901 cr · ~1,258 cr shares
- FY26 consolidated PAT (owners) ₹41,424 cr → EPS ₹32.9 · **P/E 7.1x** ·
  **P/B 0.73x** · dividend ₹13.25 = **5.69% yield**
- Core finding: consolidated earnings are **hump-shaped around Brent $70–80**,
  not linear in oil. SAED claws back roughly 55% of Brent above $75, and
  54.9%-owned HPCL bleeds above ~$85 (Q1 FY27 loss ₹11,526 cr, debt +52% in one
  quarter). Record standalone PAT ₹17,034 cr became ₹11,899 cr consolidated.
- Bridge to ₹300 (+29%): needs **only** a re-rating 7.1x → 9x on unchanged FY26
  earnings. Bridge to ₹400: needs PAT ~₹55,900 cr (≈ FY24 peak ₹57,101 cr) at 9x.
- Against it: crude production fell two years running (19.33 MMT from 19.60);
  KG-DWN-98/2 at 28,000 bpd vs 45,000 target; government raised crude SAED to
  ₹10,000/tonne on 15 Sep while *cutting* refiners' export duty — policy is
  moving against upstream.
- Invalidators: SAED ratcheting higher · HPCL compensation withheld with crude
  >$100 · a fourth straight year of production decline · KG-98/2 write-down.

### Oil India (NSE) — watchlist, thinnest verification

- Price ₹482.85 · mcap ₹81,160 cr · ~168 cr shares
- Trailing PAT ₹10,120 cr · EPS ₹60.2 · **P/E 8.02x**. Q1 FY27 PAT ₹3,630 cr
  (+91.4%) annualises to ₹14,519 cr → **5.6x**. Dividend yield 2.38%.
- Cleaner crude leverage than ONGC: its ~69%-owned NRL is a refinery, not a fuel
  retailer, so far less under-recovery drag.
- Bridge to 2× (₹966): at **11x**, needs PAT ₹14,756 cr — essentially the
  current run-rate, no growth. The whole thesis reduces to "does 11–12x happen",
  and 11x sits **above** its historical 5–10x band.
- **Could not verify: production trend, debt, NRL earnings split, FY26
  full-year accounts.** Best-looking arithmetic on the thinnest evidence — treat
  with suspicion, verify before any entry.

### GSFC (NSE) — watchlist, weak catalyst

- Price ₹162.88 · mcap ₹6,531 cr · ~39.85 cr shares · **P/E 10.2x · P/B 0.52x**
- Q1 FY27 (company disclosure): income ₹3,583.15 cr · PBT ₹205.14 cr · PAT
  ₹158.54 cr · EPS ₹3.98 · +15% YoY
- **The catch: book ≈ ₹313/share, so ROE ≈ 5.1%.** A 0.52x book multiple on a
  5% ROE is roughly fair, not cheap. Fertiliser margin just compressed to 4.09%.
- Bridge to 2× (₹326 = 1.04x book): needs ROE to roughly double to ~10%, PAT
  ≈ ₹1,306 cr, i.e. 2.06x the current run-rate. Mechanically possible via
  caprolactam spreads plus the Sikka phosphoric/sulphuric acid capex. Nothing
  yet says it is happening.
- Catalyst is undated and weak. This is the reason it is watchlist, not buy.

### Benchmark every candidate must beat

**Nifty 50 index fund (direct plan), P/E 19.74 vs 10-year median 23.31.**
Mean reversion alone is ~+18% before any earnings growth. At ₹10,000 ticket
sizes prefer the index *fund* over the ETF — no DP charges, no bid-ask spread,
no premium/discount. If a single-stock idea cannot clearly beat this on a
risk-adjusted basis, the index is the correct answer.
